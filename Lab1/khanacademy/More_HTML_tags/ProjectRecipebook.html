Recipe #1 - Traditional Manti

  • Time: 2 hours
  • Serves: 6
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 3 cups
Ground beef 500g
Onions 2 large
Water 1 cup

Step 1: Mix flour with water and salt to make dough.

Step 2: Mix ground beef with finely chopped onions.

Step 3: Roll out dough and cut into squares.

Step 4: Place meat filling in center of each square.

Step 5: Fold and seal the manti.

Step 6: Steam for 40 minutes.

Source: Traditional Recipes

Recipe #2 - Quick Manti

  • Time: 1 hour
  • Serves: 4
Ingredients Quantity
Dumpling wrappers 30 pieces
Ground lamb 300g
Onions 1 large

Step 1: Mix ground lamb with chopped onions.

Step 2: Place filling on dumpling wrappers.

Step 3: Fold wrappers and seal edges.

Step 4: Steam for 25 minutes.

Source: Quick Recipes

Recipe #3 - Vegetarian Manti

  • Time: 90 minutes
  • Serves: 5
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 3 cups
Pumpkin 400g
Onions 2 medium
Butter 100g

Step 1: Make dough from flour and water.

Step 2: Grate pumpkin and mix with onions.

Step 3: Roll dough and cut into squares.

Step 4: Fill with pumpkin mixture.

Step 5: Steam for 35 minutes.

Step 6: Serve with melted butter.

Source: Vegetarian Recipes
